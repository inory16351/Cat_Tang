Read `Art/tools/specs/_village_common.md` first and follow it exactly (materials, richness rules, coordinates, export, preview).

These pieces make the EDGE and SURROUNDINGS of the floating island. The flat walkable top of the island is made by the game (a ground mesh at height 0), so edge modules must have their TOP SURFACE flush at Z = 0 and blend into it.
Edge modules are placed side by side around an elliptical island outline (about 60 x 50 m), each rotated so its +Y side faces OUTWARD (toward the void). They overlap ~0.5 m with neighbours, so both ends (X = +-W/2) should be irregular rock (no flat cut faces showing).
Seen from ~55 deg above, the player sees the top lip and the outer cliff face plunging down into the void, so the UNDERSIDE matters: it should taper down into a hanging rocky root shape like a floating island (inverted cone), with layered strata, dangling roots and chunks breaking off.

Pieces:
1. `Edge_A` — width 6 m, depth 4 m (from Y=-3 inside to Y=+1 outside lip), depth below Z=0 about 9 m tapering to a point. Top: a thin layer of `sand` with tufts of dry grass blocks and a few broken `plank` boardwalk ends sticking out over the lip. Outer face: strata bands of `earth`, `rock`, `rock_dark`, `stone`; a buried broken wagon wheel (`wood_dark`, `iron`); dangling `root` strands; 3-4 rock chunks detached and falling/floating just below and outside the edge, with `sand_glow` trails crumbling away (the island is vanishing at its rim).
2. `Edge_B` — same size as Edge_A but a different variation: a fence post line along the lip with the last posts dissolving into floating fragments, a half-buried `brass` gear and a sagging `clock_face` melting over the lip (`gold_drip`), more crystals of `sand_glow` in the strata.
3. `Edge_C` — width 6 m, a rocky outcrop variant: a big boulder group on the lip (`rock`, `stone`, `rock_dark`), a small dead tree (`wood_weathered`) leaning out over the void, roots hanging deep.
4. `FloatIsle_S` — small floating islet, about 2.5 m across, top 0.6 m thick then tapering down 2.5 m: tufts, a single fence piece or a tiny cactus (`cactus`, `cactus_dark`), dangling roots, `sand_glow` crumbs. Origin at the center of its top surface.
5. `FloatIsle_M` — about 5 m across: a fragment of boardwalk with a hitching post and a fallen lantern (`lantern_glow`), rocks, roots underneath, crumbling chunks. Origin at center of top.
6. `FloatIsle_L` — about 8 m across: a broken piece of a western shack (2 walls and part of a tin roof, `wood`, `wood_dark`, `roof_tin`, `window_lit`) whose top dissolves upward into fragments; underside strata and roots ~5 m deep. Origin at center of top.
7. `Debris` — a loose cluster (about 3 x 3 x 3 m bounding box, origin at its center) of 15-25 small floating fragments: planks, shingles, gears (`brass`), clock hands (`iron`), a playing-card-sized clock numeral (`gold`), sand clumps (`sand_glow`), tiny `stardust` sparkles. Fragments get smaller toward the top.
8. `Plaza` — the round central plaza laid ON the ground (origin bottom center, top surface at Z = 0.06, total thickness ~0.12 m so it sits on the ground mesh): diameter 12 m, a ring of `stone` / `stone_dark` paving blocks, an inner ring of `plank` boards laid radially, and at the center a giant CLOCK DIAL inlay 6 m wide flush with the floor: `clock_face` disc with `brass` bezel ring, 12 `gold` numeral blocks (simple bars, no letters), two `iron` hands pointing at 11:59, and a `gold_drip` molten puddle where the dial melts at one side. 4 short boardwalk stubs leaving the plaza at N/E/S/W.
9. `Path` — a straight boardwalk path segment 4 m long x 2.4 m wide, top at Z = 0.08, `plank` / `wood` / `wood_light` / `wood_weathered` boards across, `wood_dark` side beams, `iron` nails, one or two boards missing/cracked. Ends must tile cleanly when placed in a line along Y.
