These are the 5 facility buildings of the hub village. They are FULL 3D buildings (front, both sides, back and roof all modelled — the camera looks down at ~55 deg and the player walks around them), not flat facades.
Each has a clear ENTRANCE at the front center (+Y side) at ground level: a door (closed, with `iron` hinges and a `brass` knob) under a little porch/awning, steps of 1-2 boards, and a front boardwalk strip 1.2 m deep along the front.
Each has the vanishing effect on its upper part (see common rules): the top ~25% of the false-front / roof breaks up into floating fragments rising above, with `sand_glow` trails. Keep the lower 2/3 solid and readable.

Pieces (width X x depth Y x height Z incl. floating fragments; origin bottom center of the building footprint):
1. `Inn` (the Memory Room inn, 2 stories) ~ 11 x 8 x 11 m. Hotel with a balcony on the 2nd floor across the front (railing, posts), `window_lit` windows with `paint_rose` shutters, a big false-front crest with `gold` scrolls; a large CUCKOO-CLOCK built into the crest center: little carved wooden house shape, closed tiny doors, `clock_face` dial with `brass` bezel, two pine-cone weights on chains hanging below; hanging sign board "INN" shape (just a blank carved board with `gold` frame, no text). Roof `roof_shingle`, chimney dissolving upward.
2. `GunShop` ~ 9 x 7 x 9 m. Store with a tall false front, a long `canvas` + `cloth_red` striped awning over the boardwalk, a display window with a rack of rifles silhouettes (`wood_dark` + `iron`), a big hanging sign shaped like a REVOLVER (`iron` + `wood` grip + `gold` trim) on an `iron` bracket, a sheriff star emblem (`gold`) on the crest. `roof_tin`.
3. `AmmoWorkshop` ~ 9 x 8 x 9 m. Forge / barn style: big double barn doors (front), side lean-to with an anvil and a quench barrel, a brick-and-stone chimney (`stone`, `stone_dark`, `rust` cap) breathing out frozen `sand_glow` sparks, a giant HOURGLASS sign (`brass` frame + `glass` bulbs + `sand_glow` sand) mounted above the doors, bullet-shaped finials (`brass`) on the crest, crates of cartridges by the door.
4. `Wardrobe` (tailor) ~ 8 x 7 x 9 m. Narrow pretty shop painted `paint_sage` and `cloth_cream` trim, a bay display window with two dress-form mannequins (`cloth_cream`, `cloth_red`, `wood` stand), a hanging sign shaped like a THREAD SPOOL with a needle (`wood_light`, `cloth_red` thread bands, `iron` needle), bunting of small fabric pennants (`cloth_red`, `cloth_cream`, `paint_rose`) along the eaves, a tape-measure ribbon (`gold`) curling up into the floating fragments.
5. `TimeGate` (the gate that starts a run) ~ 9 x 4 x 11 m. The opening between pillars (~4.5 m wide, ~6 m tall) is EMPTY (the player walks through it toward the void). Lanterns on hooks at each pillar; the top of the arch dissolves into floating clock gears and numerals.
