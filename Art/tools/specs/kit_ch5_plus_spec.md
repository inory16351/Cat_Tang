## Goal
The ELITE (powered-up) versions of the six chapter-5 enemies (shattered porcelain/obsidian dolls of the time prison: violet/indigo bodies, gold kintsugi cracks, mirror shards, magenta eyes). Tripo credits ran out, so the game re-uses each normal enemy's rigged body (with a darker, more cracked texture) and bolts these extra BLENDER PIECES onto its bones, plus a bigger main weapon. Design brief from the level design: "more broken, gold cracks spreading, VIOLET RIFT CRYSTALS bursting out of the body". So every piece is about jagged violet `crystal` clusters and `night_sky` rift slits with `crack_gold` rims and `mirror` shards erupting from the body, held by `gold` kintsugi clamps.

The enemies are chunky 2-head-tall chibi dolls. Design every piece for a **2.2 m tall** reference doll (the game scales by height / 2.2):
- head: centre about Z 1.65, radius ~0.42 (huge head, top ~Z 2.1); the face is on the FRONT (+Y) of the head — never cover the face.
- shoulder joints X ±0.30, Z 1.15; torso ~0.6 wide, ~0.45 deep (Y -0.22 .. +0.22), chest front at Y +0.22
- hips joint Z 0.8.

Coordinates for every ARMOR piece: Blender Z-up, **+Y = the doll's FRONT**, **+X = the doll's RIGHT side**. Each piece's ORIGIN is the ATTACH POINT given below (the game puts the origin on that bone), NOT the bottom centre. Each piece = ONE joined mesh exported to `Game/Assets/Art/Characters/Ch5/Kit_EP_<Name>.fbx`. At least 4 materials, bevels on hard edges, no detail thinner than ~0.03 m, chunky, cel-shade friendly, under ~3000 triangles each. Pieces must read clearly from a high top-down camera (~55 deg), so put most of the volume UP and OUT (above shoulders, behind the back, on top of the head), not in front of the face.
