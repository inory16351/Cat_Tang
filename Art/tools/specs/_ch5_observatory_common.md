## Common rules v2 (chapter 5 "The Collapsing End of Time" — REBUILT 2026-11-20 as the GREENWICH OBSERVATORY OF TIME)
This file REPLACES the setting / colour part of `_ch5_common.md` (keep using `_ch5_common.md` only for the richness, coordinate and export rules, and the material-name mechanism).

Setting: the final chapter is a dreamlike, collapsing ROYAL OBSERVATORY that keeps the world's STANDARD TIME (Greenwich: the place where time itself is measured and the prime meridian starts). It floats in a deep blue starry void and is breaking apart. Top-down cel-shaded roguelite, camera ~55 deg looking down, objects read at small size.
Motifs (use them, they are the identity): the 24-HOUR GATE CLOCK (a big dial with 24 tick blocks and ONE hand), the PRIME MERIDIAN (a straight brass line inlaid in walls and floors, broken and offset where the stone cracks), the RED TIME BALL on a mast, armillary spheres, great refractor telescopes, observatory domes with an open slit, orreries, celestial globes, star charts / constellation lines, regulator (long-case) clocks, sundials. Roman-numeral / tick shapes only — never readable letters.
Mood: mysterious, dreamy, grand, quiet; time is still THE subject — the instruments that measure time are cracking.
Structure language: pale stone (Portland-stone white-blue) + deep NAVY brick / panels, stacked moldings, arched windows that show the starry void, BRASS instruments and trims. Collapse: cracks glowing with cold cyan "time light" (`crack_gold` material name, the game colours it cyan), pieces broken off and HOVERING 0.2-0.5 m away (joined into the same mesh), broken top silhouettes.
