## Common rules (chapter 5 "The Collapsing End of Time" = the time prison, final chapter)
Setting: a dreamlike, COLLAPSING space at the end of time (top-down cel-shaded roguelite, camera ~55 deg looking down, objects read at small size).
The whole map is a warped, shattered space: a deep indigo-violet starry VOID, the ground and walls cracked like a broken MIRROR, with GOLD light leaking out of every crack and white starlight. Fragments of the previous chapters float and lie around, twisted and re-coloured violet/gold: a Wild-West town (wooden saloon planks, powder kegs), a train station (iron rails, luggage trunks, station clocks), a mirror desert (mirror shards, sandstone, crystal), a clock tower (gears, pendulums, dials). Motif: "COLLAPSE" — things are breaking apart, pieces hover slightly detached with gaps, gold cracks run through everything. Mood: grand, eerie, final, beautiful.
Stages fall from the outside to the centre: S1 shattered mirror threshold → S2 jumbled memory corridor (mixed fragments of chapters 1-4) → S3 drifting debris islands → S4 collapsing clock heart. Boss arena = centre of the time prison.
Colours (the game recolors by material name): VIOLET + GOLD COSMOS — deep navy-violet void, purple/lilac stone, bright gold cracks and trims, white starlight, silver-blue mirror. Enemy accents are MAGENTA. Warm only for the merchant. Give every piece clearly separated dark and light parts.
Signature accent: GOLD CRACKS (inset seams / lightning-shaped grooves filled with `crack_gold`), MIRROR SHARDS (flat angular facets in `mirror`), floating DETACHED CHUNKS (a piece broken off and hovering 0.1-0.4 m away from where it belongs — joined into the same mesh object), star dust. Chunky silhouettes, slightly hand-made irregularity (seeded random per piece, `random.seed(<fixed>)`). BEVEL modifier (0.02-0.05, 2 segments, angle limit, clamp overlap) on hard parts.

### Richness (the user wants high-end, not flat single-color objects)
- Every piece uses AT LEAST 4 role materials (walls / landmarks 6-8); no single material covers more than ~55% of the visible surface. Break big surfaces into bands, panels, insets and trims.
- Carve: stacked moldings, recessed panels, inlaid star / constellation patterns, relief clock dials, gold crack seams, faceted shard clusters, chamfered edges everywhere.
- No detail thinner than ~0.04 m, no micro noise. Budgets: props up to ~5000 tris, walls / landmarks / merchant up to ~8000.

### Materials - ALL GREY, named by ROLE
Every material is a flat Principled BSDF with base color (0.5, 0.5, 0.5), NO textures. The game recolors by material NAME, so use exactly these names (pick what you need, do not invent others):
`void_stone` (violet-grey stone), `void_stone_dark`, `marble` (pale lilac-white), `marble_dark`, `violet`, `violet_dark`, `indigo` (deep navy-violet), `night_sky` (starry void backdrop panels), `starlight` (small glowing star bits), `crack_gold` (glowing gold crack fill), `gold`, `gold_dark`, `mirror` (silver-blue mirror face), `mirror_back`, `crystal` (violet crystal), `magenta` (enemy accent / gem), `wood`, `wood_dark` (ch1 western remnants), `iron`, `iron_dark`, `steel` (ch2 station remnants), `brass`, `dial_face`, `dial_mark` (ch4 clock remnants), `sand` (ch3 desert remnants), `red_lacquer`, `cloth` (banners, rags), `lamp_warm`, `porcelain`, `velvet`.

### Coordinates / export
Blender Z-up, +Y is the FRONT (the face the player sees / the room-facing side), origin at the bottom center of each piece (unless the piece says otherwise).
Each piece = ONE joined mesh object named exactly as listed (unless the piece says it has several objects), exported to its own FBX `Game/Assets/Art/Characters/Ch5/Kit_<Name>.fbx` with
`axis_forward='-Z', axis_up='Y', apply_scale_options='FBX_SCALE_UNITS', bake_space_transform=True, object_types={'MESH'}, use_mesh_modifiers=True, mesh_smooth_type='FACE', add_leaf_bones=False, bake_anim=False`.
Recalc normals outward. Clear the scene between pieces (or export selected only). No cameras/lights. Print `EXPORTED <Name> <tris> <dims>` per piece and `DONE` at the end. Create the folder `Game/Assets/Art/Characters/Ch5/` if missing.
Use `Art/tools/blender_kit_ch4_props.py` as a reference for setup / bevel / export style. Short Korean header comment at the top of the script (what it makes, how to run).
Finally render ONE preview PNG of all pieces laid out in a grid (Workbench engine, flat MATERIAL color with a distinct viewport color per material name so pieces are readable — use violet/gold/silver-ish preview colours close to the game palette, camera ~55 deg from above-front, 1800x1000) to `Art/tools/specs/<script name without .py>_preview.png`. The preview is only for review; it must not change the exported FBX files.
