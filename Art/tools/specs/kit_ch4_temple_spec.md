## Why
The chapter-4 rooms currently read like a FACTORY (iron plates, storage shelves, girders, grates). The user wants it to feel like a TEMPLE / CATHEDRAL of time inside the clock tower: a sacred, grand, quiet place where clockwork is worshipped. Think gothic cathedral nave + clock tower: arched colonnades open to a starry night, slender columns with carved capitals, hanging red banners, lanterns, rose windows that are clock faces, altars with candles. Keep the clock motif (dials, Roman-numeral bars, small gears as ornaments) but as SACRED ORNAMENT, not machinery. Noir palette: white/ivory stone and silver against inky black, red only for banners/ribbons/small gems.
Use mostly `stone`, `stone_dark`, `porcelain` (white marble), `silver`, `iron_dark`, `dial_face`, `dial_mark`, `night_sky`, `starlight`, `ribbon_red`, `red_lacquer`, `lamp_warm` (tiny, lanterns only), little `brass`.
